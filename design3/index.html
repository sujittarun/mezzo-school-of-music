The musical set

The Dues tab and the Month tab stay as they are. The Score was creatively right and operationally overboard — a screen you admire is not the same as a screen you open every afternoon at five past three with a child standing in front of you.

So here are two more attendance ideas that are plainly simple — both are a list, which is the least surprising thing an attendance screen can be — and musical in what the list is made of rather than in how it is arranged.

The tab is now called Attendance, in all four prototypes and in the real app. All of these are live — tap them in the previews.
